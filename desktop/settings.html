Starting your integrated research workspace…

OcWorkspace is included with OpenConstruction and starts automatically. On first launch, the app prepares its research tools; keep an internet connection available until setup finishes.

  1. Open Models and add your model provider, model ID and API key.
  2. In Agent & tools, choose that model as your global default.
  3. Create a research project and start a session.
